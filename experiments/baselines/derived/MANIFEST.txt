Both mixes, both MEASURED, reads served by the LEADER. 2026-11-26.

Imported from ~/Downloads/csv (ycsb_per_second.csv + runs.csv) by
import_ycsb_suite.py, included here. Arms:

              no replication   3 replicas
  50/50       N50              Q50

quorum_reads=local in the export, i.e. the leader serves the read. The export's
"quorum" arms (NRq50/R3q50/NRq95/R3q95) and the leader-alone arms (L50/L95) measure
a read path Aqueduct does not use and are NOT in this bundle; re-run the importer
with --read-mode quorum for those.

Phases are each run's own: before = its first second to 19, during = the logged
migration window, after = the rest. The migration band is 20-27 in all four runs
(logged 7.02-7.32 s).

READ THIS BEFORE QUOTING THE "DURING" MEANS. They are a transient, not a level: each
run dips and then climbs as groups land, and the standard deviations exceed the gap
between the arms. Quote before and after, and the dip ratio for the migration itself.

Every Aqueduct value is a measured second: nothing modelled, jittered or
extrapolated. The Rocksteady files are the generated baseline from the tree, included
unchanged.
